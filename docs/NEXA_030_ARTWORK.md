# Shared Nexaween pumpkin artwork

Latest follow-up: [Home-only visible candle flicker](NEXA_030_HOME_CANDLE.md) supersedes the earlier shared flicker behavior described below.

The supplied `Whimsical Glowing Pumpkin at Midnight.png` is the visual reference for the shared pumpkin. Its SHA256 is `7CE0AE9F161CC79331B4C6AAE4FC0EDD9D61A1B131CD4D7B3C808076A638277B`.

The built-in imagegen tool produced the project asset [nexaween-pumpkin-030.png](../assets/nexaween-pumpkin-030.png), copied from the generated output into the repository. No CLI/API fallback was used. SHA256: `464D53F45E8164DBDA68DA78A9AB69F17B203086D8E983C32697802AA63DAE52`. The 1536×1024 RGBA PNG is 2,020,773 bytes. Alpha was inspected: transparent corner pixels, retained partial-alpha edges/glow. It contains the isolated pumpkin, stem, leaves and attached curls; the reference forest/floor are absent.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction / illustration-story. Create a production transparent PNG game/UI pumpkin asset using the attached image as the exact art reference. Keep its whimsical painterly plump dimensional rich orange pumpkin, same friendly rounded carved glowing eyes and smiling mouth, small triangular nose, face oriented toward the LEFT and slightly upward in three-quarter view. Keep a thick curved centrally rooted brown/green stem and a few designed dark-green tapered curling vines/spirals with recognizable leaves physically attached. Preserve the painted texture and gentle warm golden internal candlelight. Isolate pumpkin, stem, leaves and limited attached vine curls on a genuinely transparent background; remove entire purple forest, floating embers, ground, shadows cast onto the ground, backdrop and floor reflections. Full uncropped subject with small transparent margins, compact balanced silhouette suitable bottom-right Home UI, crisp readable scaled small. No text, no UI, no neon, no plastic, no extra objects. This will be the shared art for all pumpkin designs in the app.

`NexaPumpkin` loads one frozen bitmap and fits it at its native 3:2 aspect ratio. Home, widget-edge/background pumpkin drawing assets and pumpkin-shaped lollipop decorations use that renderer. Other candy containers retain their established candy artwork. Existing executable/logo resources are unchanged. The Home pumpkin reaches 520 DIP wide on a large window, scales down with the viewport, and reserves footer clearance. Its image and vines are stationary. A separate small, low-opacity warm light overlay fluctuates smoothly; instances use different phase offsets. Background pumpkin actors no longer participate in the candy decoration recycling/fade loop. Widget-edge extension vines retain slow gentle animation with two intentional dark-green curves rooted at the shared stem.

The generated image is an asset, not implementation evidence. The files in [media/nexa030](media/nexa030) are RenderTargetBitmap captures of real WPF windows/controls using disposable fixtures. Physical mixed-DPI appearance and subjective art approval remain open.

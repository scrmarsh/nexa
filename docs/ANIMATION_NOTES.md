# Species animation notes

Nexa uses stylized vector animation. The anatomy and gait constraints below guide it; it does not simulate exact joint dynamics, wingbeat aerodynamics or every species within a common-name group. Costumes and Combative gameplay are deliberately fantastical.

| Creature | Animation constraints |
|---|---|
| Ant | Six legs, coordinated alternating tripods, antenna probing, wingless worker form, shared candy load for paired carriers. |
| Spider | Eight legs rooted on the cephalothorax, coordinated support/swing groups, short pedipalps, no insect antennae, local web construction. |
| Ladybug | Six walking legs, separate elytra opening for flight, a single functional hindwing pair, tucked flight legs, landing/grooming. |
| Roach | Six legs, alternating support, quick turns/scuttling, long moving antennae and retreat behavior. |
| Centipede | A 15-pair stone-centipede interpretation, flexible segments, traveling leg waves and specialized long ultimate legs; no millipede-style tight spiral. Other centipede taxa have different counts. |
| Fly | Six legs, one functional wing pair and paired halteres, hovering/landing and front-leg grooming; local feeding instead of ant-style transport. |

Rapid wings use a translucent swept blur to avoid slow/flashing alias artifacts at the application's bounded frame cadence. Cartoon blinking/pupil tracking belongs only to Adorable Mode; natural compound eyes/spider eyes keep fixed features. Motion quality changes detail/cadence, not anatomy. Reduced motion uses static artwork with appropriate leg counts.

Regression checks exercise six/eight/30-leg poses, finite articulated joints, tripod coordination, spider roots, ultimate-leg behavior, shortest-angle turning, bounded gaze, resting pose, flight-wing counts, carrying conservation and persistence, local fly feeding, effect caps, stopped/disposed animation timers and real-operation notification states. A 48-frame native-renderer preview is generated by `--animation`.

Research anchors:

- [High-speed locomotion in the Saharan silver ant — Journal of Experimental Biology](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/222/20/jeb198705/224418/High-speed-locomotion-in-the-Saharan-silver-ant): insect gait coordination reference; the renderer does not reproduce the paper's measured speeds.
- [Spider locomotion kinematics — primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6935789/): coordinated spider joint/leg motion.
- [Ladybird hindwing folding — PNAS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28507159/): distinguish protective elytra from functional hindwings.
- [Fly flight and halteres — primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4321282/): functional wing/haltere distinction.
- [Multilegged locomotion — PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2213698120): traveling leg coordination as a locomotion reference.
- [Centipede ultimate legs — primary anatomical study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6330759/): specialized terminal appendages rather than another ordinary walking pair.

Remaining refinements: surface-conforming foot placement, articulated load lifting, more detailed joint chains/antennae, and richer species-specific construction/social choreography. The tested implementation is a bounded 2D approximation.

**The first public Nexa Beta is here.** Build a little space for your big ideas—and invite a few eight-legged friends along for the ride.

## 🕸️ Welcome to Nexaween

A space for whatever **haunts** you next.

- **A desktop with a little personality.** Seasonal colors, playful spiders, pumpkins and Halloween decorations bring your workspace to life. Tune the appearance, motion and companions in Settings.
- **Your Space, your layout.** Arrange movable widgets for notes, checklists, clocks, counters, Scripture, flashcards and the Pixel Timer. Build around the way you work.
- **Sessions worth coming back to.** Start a focus session, capture what happened, and return to saved recaps and History. Session completion now has a clearer confirmation and return flow.
- **Light or dark, comfortably.** Nexa Default Home now has a lighter background and dark logo lettering in Light mode, with the original charcoal look in Dark mode.
- **A smoother Windows welcome.** The installer follows your PC's light/dark preference, has sharper branding and separate Desktop/Start Menu checkboxes, and can repair missing or damaged application files.
- **Keep your work close.** Local saves, recovery, verified migration and credential-free backup exports help you keep your Spaces and history together.

## 🚀 Make your first Space

1. Download **NexaSetup.exe** above and run the installer.
2. Pick the shortcuts you want, then open Nexa.
3. Choose **Create New Space**, add a few widgets, and make it yours.
4. Explore **Settings → Appearance** for Light, Dark, System and Nexaween options.

**Already using an Alpha build?** Close Nexa and run the new installer. Existing saves are preserved; original legacy copies are retained during migration. A backup export is available in Settings if you want a copy of your work before updating.
